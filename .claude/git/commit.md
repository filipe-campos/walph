## Use the patterns below when making commits

Summary in English, lower case, imperative, no trailing period. Scope = area
(`explorer`, `editor`, `search`, `git`, `terminal`, `ipc`, `ui`, `build`, `claude`).

**Feature**
feat({{scope}}): [summary of the feature]

**Bugfix**
fix({{scope}}): [summary of the bugfix]

**Hotfix**
hotfix({{scope}}): [summary of the hotfix]

**Refactor / tooling / docs**
refactor({{scope}}): … · chore({{scope}}): … · docs({{scope}}): …
